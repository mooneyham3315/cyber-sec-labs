# Module 01: Introduction to Information Security

**Path:** Junior Cybersecurity Analyst (HTB Academy)  
**Status:** Completed  

## Core Concepts Covered
* **CIA Triad:** Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability — the foundational pillars of information security.
* **Security Roles:** Understanding the operational differences between Red Team (offensive security), Blue Team (defensive security / SOC), and Purple Team (collaborative improvement).
* **Risk Management:** Identifying assets, assessing threats and vulnerabilities, and applying controls to mitigate risk.
* **Security Frameworks & Compliance:** Overview of industry standards (NIST, ISO 27001) and regulatory requirements.

## Key Takeaways
* Defense requires understanding offensive methodologies; effective threat hunting starts with knowing how attackers enumerate and exploit systems.
* Documentation and structured reporting are just as critical as technical execution in any security role.
